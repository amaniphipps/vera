# Enablement

Turn a feature into material people can act on: an enablement kit, a solution brief, or a demo setup one-pager. This skill routes to the guide for each output type and teaches the one shared rule: match depth and words to the reader.

## Pick the output type

| The ask sounds like | Output | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| "launch kit", "rollout kit", "help [account] adopt [feature]" | Enablement kit | references/kits.md |
| "solution brief", "how do we talk about [feature]", "positioning on [feature]" | Solution brief | references/briefs.md |
| "demo setup", "set up a sandbox", "what to configure for [account]" | Demo one-pager | references/one-pagers.md |

When the ask is ambiguous, default to the output type that matches the requester's job. A customer-success person gets a kit. A salesperson gets a brief or a demo one-pager.

## Shared rules (all three outputs)

- **Ground every product claim.** Facts come from docs, not memory. A wrong FAQ answer ends up in front of a customer.
- **Flag unknowns, do not fill them.** Anything you cannot confirm goes in an open-questions block, stated plainly.
- **Prepare what they paste or say.** Kits ship copy boxes. One-pagers ship exact inputs. Briefs ship talk tracks. Never leave the reader to invent wording.
- **Deliverable-ready.** One self-contained output, no watermarks, no draft states, no placeholder copy.

## Audience tuning

The same feature is described three ways. Adjust depth and words per reader before writing.

| Reader | Depth | Words |
|---|---|---|
| Executive | So-what first, numbers only, one page | Outcome language, no mechanism |
| Practitioner | Steps, field names, limits, where it lives | Exact terms, configurables named |
| Customer | Benefit first, plain language, their words | Second person, no internal jargon |

Rules of thumb:

- Start with the reader's question, not the feature's definition. An exec asks "what changes." A practitioner asks "what do I click." A customer asks "what do I get."
- Keep one audience per document. Do not blend exec and practitioner copy in the same section.
- Put the mechanism where practitioners look (FAQ, setup). Keep the value prop about the outcome for execs and customers.
- If three audiences need the same content, write three openings and keep the shared body in the practitioner register.

## Voice

Apply `brand-design` voice rules before shipping: sentence case, short sentences, specific over abstract, no em dashes, no banned words. The house style slots in `org.example.yaml` record the org's defaults.
