# Visuals

Deliver clear visuals. This skill routes to three guides depending on what you are making.

| You need to | Read |
|---|---|
| Architecture, flow, or sequence diagram | references/excalidraw.md |
| A mockup of a real product screen | references/mockups.md |
| Design structure or tokens from a Figma file | references/figma.md |

## Shared rules

- Production is the ground truth. When a real product or design system exists, match it. Do not invent a visual language.
- Verify the output. Render it and look at it before delivery. Export to PNG/SVG and inspect, or open in a browser and click through.
- Deliverable-ready. Output is ready to forward as-is, with no leftover demo copy or placeholders.
